## What it demonstrates

A clickable first draft of Juno PM's core workflow: taking messy, unstructured product signals such as interview transcripts, support tickets, and executive emails and transforming them into structured product insights and a draft Opportunity Brief. The prototype demonstrates the basic flow from raw input → AI synthesis → PM-ready output.
_____

## Debrief

- **What worked:**
The three-column layout makes the value proposition easy to understand at a glance. The progression from raw signals to structured insights to a draft PRD feels intuitive and gives Juno a clear role in the PM workflow. The processing/loading state also helps communicate that Juno is actively analyzing the input rather than simply displaying static content.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:**
The biggest limitation is that the AI output is currently simulated rather than actually analyzing the input. Because of this, the experience can feel more like a UI mockup than a true AI product. The structured insights are also fairly basic and don't yet show the evidence or reasoning behind why Juno identified something as important.

- **What I'd change next pass:**
I would adapt the prompt to ask it to parse out the information. Without limited Lovable credits, I would do an AI based analysis, but because of my limited credits, I would add local demo logic. I would make the AI output more grounded in the source material by showing the evidence behind each insight and making the connection between individual signals and the resulting Opportunity Brief clearer. I would also explore how Juno can move beyond synthesis into prioritization and recommendations while keeping the PM in control of higher-stakes decisions.
