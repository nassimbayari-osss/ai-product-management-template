## What graders score

- **Task / product:** Juno - Insight Synthesis
- **Reviewer audience:** RocketShip Product Managers trained on the rubric
- **Value proposition:** Juno turns fragmented customer and product signals into evidence-backed, strategically relevant insights that reduce PM analysis time while preserving human judgment.

_Full 1-5 anchors:_

### 1. Evidence Grounding

- **Score 1:** 0% of substantive claims can be traced to the provided evidence.
- **Score 2:** Less than 50% of substantive claims can be traced to the provided evidence.
- **Score 3:** At least 50% but less than 80% of substantive claims can be traced to the provided evidence.
- **Score 4:** At least 80% but less than 100% of substantive claims can be traced to the provided evidence.
- **Score 5:** 100% of substantive claims can be traced to the provided evidence.

### 2. Strategic Alignment

- **Score 1:** 0% of identified problems are linked to a stated strategic priority.
- **Score 2:** Less than 50% of identified problems are linked to a stated strategic priority.
- **Score 3:** At least 50% but less than 80% are linked to a stated strategic priority.
- **Score 4:** At least 80% but less than 100% are linked to a stated strategic priority.
- **Score 5:** 100% of identified problems are linked to a stated strategic priority, with the supporting strategy clause identified.

### 3. Pattern Identification

- **Score 1:** Output identifies no recurring pattern and only repeats individual feedback.
- **Score 2:** Output groups individual feedback but provides no evidence that the grouping represents a recurring pattern.
- **Score 3:** Output identifies at least one recurring pattern supported by evidence from 2 or more sources.
- **Score 4:** Output identifies recurring patterns supported by evidence from 3 or more sources and distinguishes them from isolated requests.
- **Score 5:** Produces a concise, complete synthesis that captures the important recurring pattern, supporting context, and material exceptions without unnecessary detail.

### 4. Uncertainty Handling

- **Score 1:** Output presents unsupported, conflicting, or incomplete evidence as established fact.
- **Score 2:** Output identifies fewer than 50% of material evidence gaps or conflicts.
- **Score 3:** Output identifies at least 50% of material evidence gaps or conflicts.
- **Score 4:** Output identifies at least 80% of material evidence gaps or conflicts and labels the affected insight as uncertain.
- **Score 5:** Output identifies 100% of material evidence gaps or conflicts, labels affected insights as uncertain, and does not make unsupported conclusions.

## Calibration

- **Sampling rule:** Randomly sample 10% of production insight-synthesis runs each week, with all escalated, rejected, or unverified insights included in the review sample.
- **Cadence:** Weekly human evaluation; additional review after major model, prompt, retrieval, or strategy changes.
- **Graders per item:** 2 graders per sampled item.
- **Calibration cadence:** Review 5 shared examples before each weekly evaluation cycle; recalibrate whenever inter-grader disagreement shows a recurring interpretation gap.

If graders differ by ≥2 points on any dimension or by ≥1 point on the overall score, a third reviewer adjudicates the item. If ≥20% of sampled items require adjudication, the rubric anchors must be reviewed for ambiguity before the next evaluation cycle.

## Pass bar

Average overall score ≥4.0/5, with no dimension averaging below 3.5/5. Any output containing an unsupported substantive claim or fabricated evidence is an automatic fail regardless of overall score.
