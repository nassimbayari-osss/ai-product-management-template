## Entry point

**Signal type:** New message / transcript received

A new customer interview transcript or product-signal batch is submitted to Juno for strategic-alignment analysis.

**What they see instantly**

“Juno is analyzing your product signals…” status appears immediately, with progress shown as Juno retrieves strategy and supporting evidence.

## The flow

1. Retrieve relevant strategy clauses using search_strategy().
2. Retrieve supporting customer/product evidence using read_tickets().
3. Synthesize and rank signals against the retrieved strategy, decision rules, and evidence.
4. Verify each recommendation is grounded in a strategy clause returned during the current request.
2. “Reading Strategy One-Pager…” → “Checking customer evidence…” → “Evaluating strategic alignment…” → “Verifying recommendations…”
3. No router required for V1. The strategic-alignment flow follows a single path: retrieve strategy → retrieve evidence → synthesize → verify → present.

## AI moments

**Placement:** Full-Page Canvas

A prioritized recommendation workspace showing P0–P3 priority cards with strategic-pillar alignment, supporting evidence, rationale, confidence, and expandable strategy citations. Each recommendation includes Review/Approve controls; unverified recommendations are clearly labeled and cannot be committed to the roadmap.

Automation + Insights → Juno does the work of synthesizing fragmented signals and produces a review-ready prioritization workspace, while the PM retains judgment over the final roadmap decision.

## Fallbacks

**Kill switch**

The PM can reject or manually edit any proposed priority before roadmap commitment. A rejected recommendation returns to Juno as feedback for another analysis cycle; a human confirmation is always required before write_roadmap() executes.

**Training signal**

PM approval, rejection, or priority edit is logged as a human decision and correction for the current workflow. V1 does not automatically use these decisions as persistent model memory or retraining data.

**Fail-safe**

If strategy retrieval or verification fails, Juno does not present the recommendation as strategically grounded. It shows an “Unverified” warning, explains the missing evidence, and blocks write_roadmap() until a valid supporting strategy clause is retrieved and verified.
